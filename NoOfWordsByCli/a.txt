split(' ')

splits on exactly one space character.
so,
hello     world

and inspect:

console.log(words)

You’ll probably see something like:

['hello', '', '', '', 'world']

Instead of:

split(' ')

you can pass a pattern:

split(/\s+/)

Now let’s decode it slowly.

/ /

means:

this is a regex pattern

\s

means:

whitespace
(space, tab, newline)

+

means:

one or more

So:

split(/\s+/)

means:

split wherever one or more whitespace characters appear

Now:

hello     world

becomes:

["hello", "world"]

instead of:

["hello", "", "", "", "world"]
